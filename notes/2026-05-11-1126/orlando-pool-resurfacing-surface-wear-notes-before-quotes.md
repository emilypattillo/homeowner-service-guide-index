# Orlando pool resurfacing: surface wear notes before asking for quotes

Pool surface problems are easier to describe when the homeowner separates staining, rough plaster, hollow-sounding spots, popped aggregate, and cracks that change water level. Photos taken in consistent light, plus a note about pool age and the last resurfacing date, can keep the first conversation focused on whether this is cosmetic wear, a preparation issue, or a leak-related concern.
