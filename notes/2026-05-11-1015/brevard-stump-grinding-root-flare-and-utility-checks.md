# Brevard stump grinding: root flare, utility, and re-sprout checks

For Space Coast homeowners, the useful pre-quote photos are usually wider than the stump itself: the base/root flare, any surface roots that cross turf or walks, and the nearest irrigation heads or utility markers. After a tropical rain pattern, it is also worth noting whether the stump area stays soft, because heavy equipment access and cleanup can change.
