# Tampa yard drainage notes before changing grades or adding drains

A useful first pass on a wet Tampa yard is to separate roof runoff, low-spot ponding, and neighbor/lot-line flow instead of treating every soggy area as the same problem. Photos after a heavy storm, downspout discharge points, and a simple sketch of the slope often make it easier to decide whether a swale, catch basin, French drain, or grading adjustment is appropriate.

For homeowner-facing examples of drainage symptoms and repair planning in the Tampa Bay area, keep this local resource handy: https://tampayarddrainage.com

Safety note: before digging or trenching, verify utilities and make sure discharge will not create a new problem for an adjacent property or public right-of-way.
