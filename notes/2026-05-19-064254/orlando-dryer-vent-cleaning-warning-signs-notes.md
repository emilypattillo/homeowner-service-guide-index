# Orlando dryer vent warning signs homeowners can document

Dryer vent issues in Orlando homes often show up as practical symptoms: clothes taking longer to dry, a laundry room that feels unusually warm, lint collecting around the outside termination, or a dryer that shuts off before the cycle finishes.

Useful details to gather before a callback include dryer location, whether the vent exits through the roof or wall, how long the run appears to be, whether birds or screens are near the outside hood, and whether the appliance has recently been moved.

Homeowners should avoid guessing that every slow-dry issue is only the duct. Airflow, lint restriction, crushed flex hose, roof termination buildup, and appliance condition can overlap, so the callback should focus on symptoms and access first.
