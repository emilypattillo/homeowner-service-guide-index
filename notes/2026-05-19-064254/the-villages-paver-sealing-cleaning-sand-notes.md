# The Villages paver sealing notes before cleaning and sand touch-up

Driveway and patio pavers around The Villages can look uneven after a season of irrigation, afternoon rain, golf-cart traffic, and normal sand movement. Sealing is easier to evaluate when the homeowner separates appearance issues from settling, edge restraint movement, or drainage concerns.

Before requesting help, note whether the pavers are dull, chalky, stained, white/hazy, loose at the edges, or missing joint sand. Photos in sun and shade are useful because sealer failure, efflorescence, mildew, and irrigation staining can look similar in one image.

The safest first step is not to promise a finish or price from a web page. A paver professional should confirm cleaning needs, sand touch-up, existing sealer condition, and whether any repair is needed before sealing.
