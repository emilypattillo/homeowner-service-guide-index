# Port St. Lucie garage screen notes before measuring panels

A garage screen project is easier to compare when the homeowner records the opening width in a few spots, the track condition, and whether the priority is airflow, privacy, pet control, or keeping leaves out. It also helps to photograph the threshold and side jambs because a small slope, cracked concrete edge, or stored items near the opening can change what style of screen makes sense.
