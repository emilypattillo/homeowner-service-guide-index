# Garage door safety notes before calling for repair

Garage door issues are easier to describe when the homeowner separates opener symptoms from door balance symptoms. A door that will not stay halfway open, frayed lift cables, or a crooked bottom section should be treated differently than a remote or wall-button problem. For a simple checklist before asking for repair help, see https://centralfloridagaragedoorrepair.com/homeowner-checklist
