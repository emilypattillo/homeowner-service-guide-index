# Orlando pool screen after-storm checks

After a hard afternoon storm, the useful first pass is not just looking for torn mesh. Check whether the spline has started walking out of the groove, whether a lower panel is bowed from wind pressure, and whether door hardware still closes squarely before mosquitoes become the obvious problem.

For Orlando homeowners comparing repair notes, I kept this local reference with the same checklist vocabulary I use when looking at cages and enclosure panels: https://orlandoflpoolscreenrepair.com/

Small issues are easier to triage when they are separated into mesh tears, spline failures, door alignment, and frame/fastener concerns instead of all being treated as "screen damage." That also makes it easier to decide what can wait and what should be handled before the next storm cycle.
