# Orlando dryer vent warning signs for homeowners

Topic: long dry times, lint buildup, roof exits, and fire-risk prevention.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Long dry times in humid weather are easy to blame on the appliance, but a restricted vent is often the simpler issue.
- Check exterior flaps while the dryer is running; weak airflow or stuck louvers point to a blockage.
- For roof-exit vents, document the vent route and avoid pushing lint farther into the line with short consumer brushes.
