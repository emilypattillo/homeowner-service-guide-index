# Lee County pool screen storm-damage checklist

Topic: pool cage panels, spline, doors, and wind damage triage.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Check high panels from the deck and avoid climbing the cage; missing spline can let a small tear become a full panel failure.
- Inspect door closers and latch alignment after wind events because frame movement can show up at the door first.
- Take wide photos plus closeups of torn mesh, bent kick plates, and loose spline before requesting repair pricing.
