# Winter Garden sprinkler zone troubleshooting notes

Topic: broken heads, valve zones, sandy soil, and coverage gaps.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Run one zone at a time and mark heads that bubble, mist, or fail to rise fully.
- Sandy Central Florida soil can hide leaks until turf browning or unusually wet spots appear.
- Check whether coverage gaps follow a blocked nozzle, tilted head, or pressure issue before changing the timer.
