# Cape Coral boat lift storm-season repair notes

Topic: cables, bunks, motors, and post-storm lift checks.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Photograph cable wraps on each drum before a storm so a repair tech can spot uneven winding later.
- Listen for motor strain when lifting under load; stopping early can prevent bent cradle parts or snapped cables.
- After heavy wind, inspect bunks and guide posts before running the lift with the boat on it.
