# Orlando roof cleaning soft-wash notes

Topic: algae streaks, tile/shingle care, and plant protection.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Black streaks are usually algae, not dirt; aggressive pressure can shorten roof life, especially on older shingles.
- Ask how landscaping will be pre-wet, rinsed, and protected during a soft-wash treatment.
- Photograph stained slopes and shaded areas so quotes compare the same roof sections.
