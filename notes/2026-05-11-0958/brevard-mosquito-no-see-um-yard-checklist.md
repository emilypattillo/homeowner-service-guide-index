# Brevard County mosquito and no-see-um yard checklist

Topic: standing water, screened patios, and coastal no-see-um pressure.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Walk the yard after afternoon storms and dump water from saucers, buckets, toys, tarps, and boat covers.
- Check lanai screen seams and door sweeps; no-see-ums can exploit gaps that look too small for mosquitoes.
- Note shaded vegetation against the house because humid plantings can keep resting areas active between service visits.
