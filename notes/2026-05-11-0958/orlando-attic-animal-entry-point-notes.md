# Orlando attic animal entry-point notes

Topic: soffits, roof returns, droppings, and exclusion planning.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Listen for timing: raccoons, squirrels, and rodents often make different patterns of noise at dawn, dusk, or overnight.
- Photograph soffit gaps, roof returns, and chewed vents from the ground for the removal company.
- Do not seal an opening until the animal is confirmed out; trapping wildlife inside creates a worse problem.
