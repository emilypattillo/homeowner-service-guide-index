# Punta Gorda seawall erosion observation notes

Topic: cap cracks, soil loss, tiebacks, and waterfront property monitoring.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Track new soil depressions behind the wall after heavy rain or high-water events.
- Photograph cap cracks with a ruler or coin for scale so changes are easier to compare month to month.
- Avoid filling voids casually before a seawall pro evaluates whether erosion is active behind the wall.
