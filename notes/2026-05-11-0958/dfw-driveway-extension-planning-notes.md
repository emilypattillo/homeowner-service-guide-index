# DFW driveway extension planning notes

Topic: clay soil movement, drainage, and practical driveway width planning.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Plan where roof and yard runoff will go before adding concrete; North Texas clay soil makes ponding problems more obvious over time.
- Measure door swing, trash-bin movement, and trailer angles rather than sizing the pad from vehicle width alone.
- Ask how the new edge will be tied into the existing driveway and whether soil prep changes near sprinkler lines.
