# Phoenix and Scottsdale artificial turf heat and drainage notes

Topic: desert heat, pet areas, infill, and drainage base prep.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Ask about turf temperature, shade patterns, and infill choices before selecting a product for kids or pets.
- Drainage base prep matters in monsoon bursts even when the yard is dry most of the year.
- For pet turf, plan rinse access and deodorizer maintenance instead of treating all turf products as the same.
