# Naples boat lift cable wear checklist

Topic: salt exposure, cable frays, switches, and lift safety.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Look for meat-hook frays with gloves, not bare hands; even a few broken strands justify a closer inspection.
- Rinse salt spray from exposed hardware when appropriate and keep electrical controls dry and covered.
- If the cradle rises unevenly, stop and document the low corner rather than trying to level it under load.
