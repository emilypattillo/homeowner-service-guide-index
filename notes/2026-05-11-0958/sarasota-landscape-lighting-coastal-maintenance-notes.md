# Sarasota landscape lighting coastal maintenance notes

Topic: corrosion, transformer load, fixture placement, and salt-air exposure.

This note is a homeowner-oriented reminder, not a contractor profile, business citation, review, or license claim.

## Practical checks

- Salt air can corrode fixtures and connections faster near the coast, so inspect finishes and wire nuts seasonally.
- Trim vegetation around path lights before assuming a dim area needs more fixtures.
- Keep transformer access clear and note which zones trip or flicker after irrigation runs.
