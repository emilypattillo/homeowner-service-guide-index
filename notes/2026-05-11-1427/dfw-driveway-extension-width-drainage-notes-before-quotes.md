# DFW driveway extension notes before sketching a wider parking area

A driveway extension is easier to discuss when the homeowner separates parking width, turn-in angle, drainage direction, sprinkler/head conflicts, and the edge detail where new concrete will meet turf or existing pavement. A quick sketch with measurements and photos after rain can prevent the first estimate from becoming only a square-footage conversation.

For a simple DFW homeowner checklist to organize photos, measurements, and questions before asking for help, see https://dfwdrivewayextensions.com/homeowner-checklist
